Picks update automatically several times a week (NFL Thu/Sun/Mon, college Sun/Tue/Thu/Sat). 1u = 1% of bankroll. Win chance is the model's estimate. Prices are the best at DraftKings or FanDuel when the pick was made and may have moved since. Full detail: NFL dashboard · college dashboard. NFL injuries come from the official injury report and IR lists, weighted by each player's recent snaps; the number is points off that team's projected margin. A quarterback change is priced through the starters' ratings, so it shows who is starting instead. College injury news is read from the web before each run. A backup quarterback's first start takes a point off his team and off the total; from his second start on it's already in the line. Other college starters missing their first game take half a point off their team (0.2 if questionable, at most 1.5), and move the total down (offense) or up (defense) by as much: educated guesses, since there's no college injury history to test them.
